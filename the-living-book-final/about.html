ABOUT

A book that can keep growing.

The Living Book is designed as a home for ideas rather than a single linear story. New webpages can be added without losing the older ones.

A simple rule

Every page gets its own reading space. Versions can preserve different stages of the work, while Contents gives the reader a clear way to move through everything that exists.

Built to evolve

This first structural prototype focuses on comfortable navigation. The writing, imagery, animations and deeper visual language can evolve later without changing the underlying book.
